# TashOS

TashOS is a modern management platform designed to help companies organize people, responsibilities, and tasks in a clear and structured way.

The goal of TashOS is to reduce operational friction in growing teams by making responsibilities transparent and prioritizing what truly matters.

---

## Vision

Growing companies often struggle with unclear responsibilities, scattered tasks, and inefficient communication.  
TashOS addresses this challenge by creating a structured, visual system for team organization and task management.

Our mission is to turn internal organization into a strategic advantage.

---

## Core Features

- Clear visualization of team structures
- Responsibility mapping across teams
- Task prioritization and assignment
- Real-time overview of operational workflows
- Reduced dependency on email communication
- Structured collaboration between teams and managers

---

## Target Users

TashOS is designed for:

- Growing startups
- Companies with multiple teams
- Organizations managing multiple locations
- Managers responsible for operational efficiency
