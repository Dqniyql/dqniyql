## TashOS: from managing tasks to managing impact

**The problem.** Small and mid-sized companies run on a patchwork of disconnected tools for tasks,
time tracking, HR and reporting. Managers spend hours every week piecing together what is actually
happening in their business, and still decide on gut feeling.

**The solution.** TashOS brings people, responsibilities and work into one platform, with an AI layer
on top that turns everyday operational data into concrete recommendations. It answers
"what should happen next?", not just "what happened?".

### What is already built

| Area | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Work management** | Tasks, teams, branches, calendar with ICS subscription, internal messenger |
| **Time tracking** | Working-time recording designed for German labour-law requirements |
| **People** | Employee profiles, monthly performance reviews, yearly reports |
| **AI insights** | Capacity analysis, deadline-risk detection, weekly insights, executive analysis, AI assistant |
| **Enterprise readiness** | Multi-company access, role-based permissions, audit and login logging, GDPR data export and deletion |

### Business model

Simple per-seat SaaS pricing that scales with the customer:

| Company size | Price per employee / month |
|---|---|
| up to 10 employees | €15 |
| 11–50 employees | €14 |
| 51+ employees | €12 |

Annual billing gets a 10% discount. Price and AI capacity depend only on headcount, which makes
the model predictable for customers and easy to forecast for us.

### Why now

- AI has become good enough to give managers useful, explainable recommendations.
- SMBs are under cost pressure and want one tool instead of five.
- A European, GDPR-first product has a clear trust advantage in the German-speaking market.
